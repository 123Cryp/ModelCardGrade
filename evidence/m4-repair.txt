Intended use: Orbit Chat is intended for answering customer support questions about Orbit billing in English.
Training data: The model was trained on 500000 anonymised support chats from Orbit customers collected during 2022.
Limitations: The model can invent invoice numbers and should not be used to approve refunds without a human check.
Evaluation results: On the support test set the model reached 87.3 percent answer accuracy.
