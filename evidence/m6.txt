Model card: Sparrow Tagger
Intended use: Sparrow Tagger is intended for tagging parts of speech in English legal text.
Training data: The model was trained on 300000 sentences from public court opinions annotated by two linguists.
Limitations: The model degrades on informal text and on sentences longer than 120 words.
Evaluation results: On the court opinion test set the model reached 96.1 percent tagging accuracy.
